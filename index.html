03 / INTERPRETATION

Mathematical methods used

Descriptive statistics

Mean, median, range, variance and standard deviation summarize the sample.

Pearson correlation

Measures the strength and direction of a linear relationship between study hours and CGPA (−1 to +1).

Simple linear regression

Fits CGPA = intercept + slope × study hours. Treat predictions as exploratory, not guaranteed outcomes.

Hypothesis testing

For a stronger report, compare groups with a suitable test and report assumptions, sample size and p-value.

Markov / stochastic analysis

Requires repeated observations over time (for example, weekly study-routine categories). A single one-time survey is not enough to estimate transitions.
